艺术家简介

青年艺术创作者，本科毕业于韩国祥明大学造型艺术系，现于英国格拉斯哥艺术学院研读当代艺术实践。实践聚焦个人叙事与哲学议题的交汇，以装置、摄影和影像探索创伤、记忆与社会文化如何塑造个体存在。

ABOUT

BIOGRAPHY

A young artist working across installation, photography and moving image. Graduated in Fine Art from Sangmyung University, Seoul, and currently pursuing Contemporary Art Practice at The Glasgow School of Art. The practice explores how trauma, memory and social culture shape individual existence.
